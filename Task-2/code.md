Description of Online Shopping Website

The ShopEasy Online Shopping Website is a simple and user-friendly e-commerce webpage designed to provide customers with an easy online shopping experience. It displays different products along with their images, prices, ratings, and discounts.

The webpage consists of a navigation bar containing the ShopEasy logo, search bar, Home, Categories, Products, and Cart options. A promotional banner welcomes users and highlights special shopping offers with up to 50% discount.

The Product Categories section allows users to view products under Electronics, Fashion, and Accessories. The Featured Products section displays four product cards: Headphones, Smart Watch, Running Shoes, and Digital Camera. Each card contains a product image, name, customer rating, price, discount, and Add to Cart button.

The webpage also includes a footer displaying the store name and copyright information. CSS is used to design the layout, colors, backgrounds, product cards, buttons, and hover effects.

Used: HTML and CSS.
